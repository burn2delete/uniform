module effect/capability and table row, then four units per variable ID and

passed allowance. The success and source-mismatch results reported that total.

physical work reports. Replace the three measured-shape traversals with boolean
shape validation: their row/map/vector/ID checks and visitation remain, while

`pmk-header`, therefore aggregates a proof report only when present; otherwise
