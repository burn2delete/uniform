# Governance and Evolution

Semantic key: `evolution`. Owner role: language-evolution/governance maintainer;
individual unassigned. [Task authority](../../specs/evolution-workflow/tasks.md)
is the sole editable implementation list. This is governance tooling, not a
new compiler pass or an accepted implementation.

## Release outcome

Produce and validate the applicable subject-bound change, compatibility,
security, RFC, experiment, stabilization, library, namespace, registry and target
decisions under [GOV1-GOV10](../phase-17-governance-and-evolution/README.md).
Preserve rejected and superseded history, stable diagnostics, actual consumer
results and source/policy/tool provenance. The aggregate outcome remains Open.
Schema-shaped records alone cannot establish implementation or release.

## Interfaces and acceptance

Inputs are the exact governed subject and its applicable source, profile,
safety, security, package, target and conformance evidence. Outputs are the
actual decisions, retained histories and named rejection artifacts below.
Build and Release consume the corresponding artifacts under their own task
criteria. Ordinary development has no additional per-run permission gate.
All named positive and negative criteria must hold before their scoped task
can be accepted; final independent review and integration remain separate.
